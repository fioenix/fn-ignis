# Spec 011 Vietnam Consumer-Market Pilot Protocol

Status: **prepared, not executed**. This protocol pre-registers five conditions required by
FR-030. It does not authorize a social login, token, live probe, persistent migration, or
corpus deletion. The owner must approve exact accounts, surfaces, quotas, and expiry under T085
before a restricted connector is used. Record the actual questions and queries before seeing
results; do not choose a case because its signals flatter the hypothesis.

## Shared case manifest

For each case, record a case ID, consumer-market decision owner, business decision, audience,
Vietnam geography, time window, core hypothesis, at least two alternatives, null hypothesis,
falsifiers, kill criteria, revision rule, required and optional social surfaces, query families,
exclusions, sample cap, allowed auth tier, quota cap, retention and redaction plan, and terminal
stop condition. Confirm the Brief and manifest before collection. Freeze the plan digest.

Use one current mission frame per case. Record every requested channel outcome, query timestamp,
observation provenance, qualification status, contradiction role, frame digest, Claim Ledger
status, Gap Report if any, and the exact user-verifiable next action. Never count an inaccessible
channel as zero demand or import a purchased dataset as primary evidence.

## Five pre-registered conditions

| Case | Planned required channels | Setup and intended stress | Required outcome and stop condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 — sufficient multi-source | `youtube`, `tiktok` | A consumer-market decision with independent social surfaces and source-specific support and contradiction probes. The actual product question is fixed before ingress. | If sufficiency passes, render only current-frame claims with both supporting and contradicting evidence. Record a bounded `proceed`, `narrow`, `reposition`, or `stop` action; stop after the confirmed plan and claim readback. If data is not sufficient, record a Gap Report instead and do not relabel the case a success. |
| P2 — required-channel authentication block | `threads` | Mark this surface decision-critical, then exercise its authentic `AUTH_REQUIRED` outcome without bypassing access controls. Do not deliberately use another person's account. | A Gap Report names the blocked surface and smallest authorized next probe; no Market verdict or Opportunity Index. Stop without trying a new credential or platform outside the manifest. |
| P3 — high volume, low relevance | `youtube` | Use a broad but pre-declared consumer query likely to return many tangential posts; apply the same relevance standard to support and contradiction. | Report raw volume separately from qualified evidence. Withhold a verdict if the qualifying frame is thin; stop when the sample cap is reached, not when a desired number of supportive posts appears. |
| P4 — contradictory signals | `youtube`, `tiktok` | Select a question with plausible competing explanations and plan explicit disconfirmation probes across the permitted surfaces. | Qualified contradiction remains visible in the Claim Ledger and artifact, conditions the action or reverses it. Stop after both roles are adjudicated; never omit inconvenient observations to improve confidence. |
| P5 — missing critical metric | `youtube` | Name a commercial decision metric in the Brief that social listening cannot directly measure, such as verified conversion, willingness to pay, or purchase frequency. | Show social observations as bounded context, withhold the missing metric and any verdict dependent on it, and recommend the smallest non-social validation step. Stop without inventing a proxy as the measured value. |

The cases are scenario classes, not claims about live Vietnam consumer markets. These planned
channel names do not authorize access. If a required channel cannot be approved under T085,
record that limitation rather than silently replacing it. Exact products, queries, source
accounts, quota costs, and observed outcomes belong in the executed results.

## Measurements and comparison

For each case measure time from confirmed Brief to qualified frame, manual analyst minutes,
percentage of rendered claims with exact eligible bindings, unsupported-verdict escapes,
distinct missing-channel states retained, contradiction visibility, and whether the decision
owner can recall one supporting and one contradicting observation. Ask the owner for decision
confidence before and after, next action, repeat-use intent, and willingness to pay; preserve
raw answers without extrapolating adoption or revenue.

Run the same frozen question and source constraints through one generic research-agent workflow
and one composable OSS workflow if both are accessible. Compare provenance, falsifier handling,
missingness, claim traceability, analyst effort, and decision usefulness. Record unavailable
comparators as unavailable; do not fabricate scores or equalize them by changing the question.

Pilot results go in `pilot-results.md` with mission IDs and exact frame identities. A safe
partial result or refusal can satisfy the evidence-integrity criteria; it is not a successful
Market verdict. Local deterministic tests are not a substitute for these real cases.
